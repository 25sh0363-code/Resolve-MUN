A Stage Worthy of the Debate

Resolve MUN 2026 will be hosted at Laurus The Universal School, Hyderabad, a premier academic campus set within a thoughtfully designed landscape of greenery and open spaces.

The campus features expansive, sunlight-filled halls and modern facilities optimized for simultaneous committee sessions, ensuring a professional and focused environment for intellectual exchange.

Strategically located for easy accessibility, the venue provides a grand stage for global diplomacy, where natural surroundings foster collaboration beyond the formal debate floor.
