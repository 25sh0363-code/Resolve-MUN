About The Conference

Where Diplomacy Meets Ambition

Resolve MUN is not just a conference. It is an arena where the next generation of global leaders converge to debate, deliberate, and drive change. Built on the pillars of intellectual rigor and diplomatic excellence, Resolve MUN challenges delegates to engage with the world's most pressing issues.

From crisis committees that demand split-second decisions to general assemblies that require sweeping coalition-building, every moment at Resolve MUN is designed to forge leaders of consequence.

MESSAGE FROM THE SECRETARIAT

A Letter from the Secretary General

Dear Delegates, Faculty Advisors, and Distinguished Guests,

It is with immense pride and a profound sense of responsibility that I welcome you to Resolve MUN 2026. In an era defined by rapid global transformation and complex geopolitical challenges, the need for diplomatic dialogue and collaborative problem-solving has never been more urgent.

At Resolve MUN, we have built a platform centered around three core pillars: Resolve, to stand firm in our commitment to justice; Reform, to adapt and improve our global systems; and Reconcile, to heal the fractures within our international community. We believe that true leadership does not solely reside in the strength of one's arguments, but in the empathy required to listen, and the courage required to bridge the divides that separate us.

Our committees have been meticulously designed to push the boundaries of traditional debate. We invite you to step beyond comfort zones, to think critically about the status quo, and to act decisively in the face of uncertainty. Over the course of three days, you will not just represent a vision; you will attempt to materialize it.

We look forward to witnessing the impact of your diplomacy. Let us embark on this journey with an open mind and a steadfast commitment to global progress.

NB
NIVEDITT BASRANI
SECRETARY GENERAL — RESOLVE MUN 2026
